Before You Begin

This is a high-stakes transfer case, not a role-play in which you are asked to imagine yourself personally responsible for nuclear war.

Privacy-first: You may use Petrov and Arkhipov throughout. No private personal example is required. If your instructor invites transfer, you may instead use a public, fictional, or low-stakes case.
Central route: Historical evidence → interpretation → warrant → standing → shared task → repair → REOPEN → Authorial Return.
1

Move 1 — Build the Evidence / Provenance Trail

What actually happened, and what are we adding as interpretation?

Historical anchors

Petrov, 1983: a Soviet early-warning system indicated what appeared to be an American missile launch. Petrov judged the warning probably false; the warning was false.

Arkhipov, 1962: aboard Soviet submarine B-59 during the Cuban Missile Crisis, a decision involving use of a nuclear torpedo required agreement among senior officers. Arkhipov did not agree.

Process-trail firewall: Historical evidence ≠ interpretation. Interpretation ≠ course framework. Course framework ≠ proven fact. Student judgment ≠ automatic authorization.

Move 2 — Reconstruct Public Reason Under Pressure

What claim, evidence, warrant, uncertainty, and standing existed when ordinary deliberation had nearly run out of time?

Standing Under Pressure

Standing firewall: Expertise ≠ total authority. Authority ≠ complete knowledge. Affectedness ≠ operational expertise.

Attribution Is Not Commitment

Commitment firewall: Attributed commitment ≠ acknowledged commitment. Showing that one justification fails ≠ proving the opposite conclusion.
3

Move 3 — Build the Adaptive Moral Commonwealth Task Space

Move outward from the heroic-person story toward system fragility, compression, and cooperation without convergence.

Visible Stability, Hidden Fragility

Misuse firewall: Visible stability ≠ proof of health. But visible stability ≠ proof of hidden fragility. The caution requires evidence either way.

The Civilizational Chiliagon — Compression Check

The problem is too large and dynamic for any unaided mind to hold directly. Models, institutions, warning systems, statistics, scenarios, and computation extend cognition — but they also compress.

Compression firewall: Simplification may be necessary. Necessary compression ≠ harmless compression. Model ≠ world.

Cooperation Without Convergence

AMC Direction Test

Connected-Doing firewall: Connected capability ≠ super-subject. Cooperation ≠ convergence.
4

Move 4 — Repair the Architecture and Specify REOPEN

Move from diagnosis to a bounded institutional intervention, then ask what could make your own repair wrong.

Do not design military technology. This is an ethics and institutional-design exercise. Work at the level of safeguards, verification, communication, authority, warning routes, dissent protection, review, and decision structure.
REOPEN firewall: Decision ≠ permanent closure. Feedback ≠ effective REOPEN unless there is a route through which feedback can change the decision.
